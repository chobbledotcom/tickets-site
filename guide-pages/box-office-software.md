    content: |
      A box office sells tickets before an event and manages the door on
      the night. Box office software is the tool that does both jobs in
      one place, so the seller is not juggling a spreadsheet, a card
      reader and a printed list.

      For a small event, the same person runs advance sales and the door.
      For a venue, the two jobs sit with different people, and the
      software is how they share one record of what was sold and who came
      in.
  - type: markdown
    dark: true
    content: |
      ## What the job needs

      - Advance sales with a capacity limit on each date, and money paid
        to the organiser's own payment account
      - A door list the staff on the night can work from, on paper or a
        screen
      - A way to add walk-up sales at the door and record cash taken
      - Check-in that flags duplicate tickets
      - Refunds that reach the payment record as well as the attendance
        list
      - Reports that record ticket income, door money and the costs of an
        event in one place, for the treasurer or accountant
  - type: markdown

      ## How Chobble Tickets covers it

      [Online ticket sales](/features/selling-tickets/) run the advance
      side, with per-date capacity and money paid straight to the
      organiser's [Stripe, Square or SumUp account](/features/stripe-and-square/).

      At the door, staff can [check tickets in with a phone camera or a
      name search](/features/qr-code-check-ins/), and duplicate scans are
      flagged. Walk-ups can be [added as manual attendees](/features/manual-attendees/),
      so the door list stays complete.

      The [ledger](/features/ledger/) records door income beside online
      sales and the costs of the event, and everything
      [exports as CSV](/features/csv-export/) for accounting.
      [Refunds](/features/refunds/) can be issued for one booking or in
      bulk, including on the night.

      ## What Chobble Tickets does not do

      It has no card terminal of its own. A card payment at the door is
      taken through the online checkout, and cash is recorded as an
      offline payment, rather than by a till integration.

      It has no reserved seating or venue seat maps, so a performance
      with assigned seats cannot be seated in the software. The
      [provider directory](/compared-to/providers/) lists the reviewed
      platforms that offer seating plans.

      Paid checkout does not reserve capacity. Capacity is used only when
      payment confirmation arrives.
