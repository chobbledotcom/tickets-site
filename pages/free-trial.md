      ## How to ask for one

      Use the [contact page on chobble.com](https://www.chobble.com/contact)
      and say you would like a free trial of Chobble Tickets.

      ## Trying it without a trial

      The [demo sites](/examples/) for arts workshops, skip hire and a play
      centre can be browsed without signing up. If you would rather start
      straight away, the [sign-up page](https://tix.chobble.com/listings)
      takes £5 for the first month of managed hosting.

      ## After the month

      Managed hosting costs £50 a year or £5 a month, paid in advance.
      Charities, community groups, artists and musicians can pay £25 a
      year on the annual plan. Chobble takes no fee from ticket sales at
      any point.
