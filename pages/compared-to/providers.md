      ## Every reviewed platform in one list

      Each row below links to a full comparison with Chobble Tickets.
      Chobble Tickets sits in the list too, highlighted in purple like on
      the [fee calculator](/compared-to/compare-all/), and it drops out of
      view like any other platform when it does not match a filter.

      Most listed platforms sell event tickets. A few are hire, delivery or
      scheduling tools that overlap with parts of what Chobble Tickets does,
      and their reviews record the same facts in the same shape. The
      filter chips describe a platform's recorded attributes, not its
      category.

      The filters read the facts recorded on each comparison page. A
      platform matches a filter only when its review records the attribute,
      so a platform whose review does not mention a fact is treated as not
      having it.

      ## How the filters work

      Every fact behind the filters is stored on the provider's comparison
      page with a review date, and the same records power this list. Reviews
      are snapshots, so a platform may have changed its features or structure
      since its review date. The comparison pages list sources and dates.

      Selecting several filters shows only the platforms whose reviews record
      all of them. [Pen and paper](/compared-to/pen-and-paper/) has a
      comparison page but no provider record, so it does not appear in the
      list.
