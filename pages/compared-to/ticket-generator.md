      # Chobble Tickets vs Ticket Generator

      Ticket Generator is a pay-as-you-go ticketing platform from Trycon, sold as prepaid ticket credits. This page compares it to Chobble Tickets.

      ## Flat-fee Ticket Generator alternative

      Chobble Tickets charges a flat £50/year or £5/month with
      [no per-ticket platform fees](/features/no-per-ticket-fees/). Charities, community
      groups, artists and musicians pay £25/year on the annual plan. Ticket prices, ticket
      volumes and feature use do not change the fee.

      Ticket Generator sells ticket credits in packs and consumes one credit per
      ticket generated. On the smallest pack the per-ticket cost is about £0.47
      ($0.60), falling to about £0.20 ($0.25) at the 10,000-credit pack. Credits
      apply to free and paid tickets alike, after the first 10 free credits.

      Because the charge is per ticket rather than per year, Ticket Generator costs
      less at very low volumes. On the smallest pack, Chobble Tickets costs less
      once you generate more than about 106 tickets a year, or around 116
      including the 10 free sign-up credits.

      ## Pricing comparison

      [Ticket Generator](https://ticket-generator.com/pricing) uses a credit model.
      One credit generates one ticket, and events with a venue layout (such as
      reserved seating) use two credits per ticket. Credit packs are priced in US
      dollars:

      - 10 credits for $6 (about $0.60 per ticket)
      - 100 credits for $50 (about $0.50 per ticket)
      - 1,000 credits for $400 (about $0.40 per ticket)
      - 10,000 credits for $2,500 (about $0.25 per ticket)

      The first 10 credits are free and added to the account on sign-up. Credits
      do not expire, but purchased credits are non-refundable.

      Creating a seating plan costs $100 or 200 credits as a one-off design
      service by the Ticket Generator team. Sending a ticket by SMS may use
      additional credits depending on the recipient's country, and some
      countries are not supported.

      Chobble Tickets is a flat £50/year or £5/month (£25/year for community
      groups, charities and schools) with no per-ticket platform fees and no
      credits to buy. You also pay Stripe, Square or SumUp processing fees on
      paid tickets. Free events cost nothing to run and issue tickets for.

      The calculator below uses the smallest pack rate (about £0.47 per ticket).
      It adds typical UK Stripe processing (1.5% + 20p) to both platforms, because
      Ticket Generator does not publish its payment-processing charge for
      collected payments separately. On free events Ticket Generator still spends
      credits while Chobble Tickets charges nothing.

      Ticket Generator is a product of Trycon, headquartered in Noida, Uttar

      QR Batch and QR API. Its site lists Deloitte, Verizon, Takeda, Google and
      the UNHCR among its customers.

      benefit. All of Chobble's code is public under
      [AGPL-3.0-only](/features/open-source/), and the platform can be
      self-hosted by anyone without depending on Chobble as a company. The
      [hosting page](/hosting/) describes its deployment options.
