      # Chobble Tickets vs Eventee

      Eventee is a conference and event app with attendee registration and engagement features. Chobble Tickets is a ticketing platform. The two overlap on registration and check-in, but solve different problems.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Open source & flat fee Eventee alternative

      Chobble Tickets is a white label ticketing platform made in the UK by a
      community interest company. Its source code is published under the
      AGPL-3.0-only licence, so anyone can read it, fork it, or run their own
      copy. Eventee does not publish its source code.

      Eventee prices by event and by annual subscription, with an attendee
      cap on each plan and no per-ticket fee. Chobble Tickets charges a flat
      £50/year or £5/month with
      [no per-ticket platform fee](/features/no-per-ticket-fees/), so the
      models differ in shape rather than in who pays per ticket. Eventee suits
      organisers running conferences and corporate events that need an
      attendee app. Chobble Tickets suits organisers selling tickets and
      collecting payments.

      ## Two products with different aims

      [Eventee](https://eventee.com) is an event app built in Brno, Czech
      Republic. An organiser builds an event in the Eventee builder, and
      Eventee publishes it as a mobile app, a web app, and a no-code event
      website. Attendees use it to follow the schedule, ask questions,
      network, and check in.

      Chobble Tickets sells tickets and collects payment. Attendees book
      through a checkout, receive a ticket with a QR code, and add it to
      Apple or Google Wallet. The organiser keeps attendee data, encrypted,
      on infrastructure they can control.

      This page compares the two to help you decide which fits your event.
      Other comparisons cover adjacent tools: [Cal.com](/compared-to/cal-com/)
      for paid scheduling, [Onfleet](/compared-to/onfleet/) for delivery, and
      [SignUpGenius](/compared-to/signupgenius/) for sign-up sheets.

      ## Pricing comparison

      Eventee sells single-event plans priced per event, plus annual
      subscription plans. The single-event prices are steep, and all exclude
      VAT and sales tax:

      | Eventee single-event plan | Price per event | Attendees per event | Team members |
      |---|---|---|---|
      | Essential | £1,299 | 200 | 3 |
      | Signature (most popular) | £2,199 | 500 | 5 |
      | Professional | £3,099 | 1,000 | 10 |

      Annual subscription plans (Team, Business, Enterprise) cover 3, 6, or
      12 events a year with attendee caps of 500, 1,000, or 3,000. Enterprise
      is quote-based. Check the [Eventee pricing page](https://eventee.com/pricing)
      for annual plan figures, which this page does not quote.

      Per-event add-ons stack on top of any plan. Each is a flat charge for one
      event:

      | Add-on (per event) | Price | Note |
      |---|---|---|
      | 100 additional attendees | £200 | For events over the plan cap |
      | Custom branding | £430 | Included on Signature and Professional |
      | Custom domain | £175 | Included on Signature and Professional |
      | Gamification | £430 | Included on Professional |
      | Groups | £430 | Included on Professional |
      | Check-in | £430 | Included on Signature and Professional |
      | Badge label printing | £430 | Included on Professional |
      | White label | £1,700 | Charged on every plan, including Professional |

      A fully-featured Professional event with white label reaches £4,799 for
      a single event, before VAT. Subscriptions bill annually and renew
      automatically, and the terms state Eventee does not offer refunds.

      Chobble Tickets charges a flat £50/year or £5/month
      ([£25/year for charities, community groups, artists and musicians](/pricing/)
      on the annual plan) with no per-ticket platform fee. Payment processing
      through Stripe, Square, or SumUp is separate. There is no attendee cap,
      no event cap, and no per-feature add-ons.

      The two price models differ in shape, not just in amount. Eventee charges
      per event and includes attendee engagement features Chobble does not
      offer. Chobble charges per year and includes paid ticket sales, encrypted
      attendee data, and self-hosting Eventee does not offer. For one paid
      event with more than a handful of tickets, Chobble's flat £50/year fee is
      a fraction of Eventee's £1,299 entry price. For attendee engagement at a
      conference, Eventee is built for that job and Chobble Tickets is not.

      ## Data ownership

      Eventee is a hosted cloud service. Attendee and event data lives on
      Eventee's servers, hosted by DigitalOcean in Amsterdam. Eventee holds an
      ISO 27001 certificate and says it does not transfer data outside the EU.
      Its privacy policy states that in no case does Eventee work with
      attendee information, and that sharing attendee data without consent is
      the organiser's responsibility. Eventee's terms grant it a licence to
      host, store, and display the content organisers upload.

      Chobble Tickets encrypts attendee data at rest, and only the organiser
      holds the private key. The platform is open source under AGPLv3, so an
      organiser can self-host it and keep data on infrastructure they control.
      If Chobble Tickets stopped operating, an organiser could run the same
      code on their own server.

      ## Who builds Eventee?

      Eventee is operated by Eventee s.r.o., a Czech limited liability company
      registered at Kopečná 940/14, 602 00 Brno, ID No. 29307236, kept by the
      Regional Court in Brno. Its terms are governed by Czech law. The about
      page lists Roman Mastalir as CEO and Petr Fiala as CTO. The about page
      does not state when the company was founded, and the sources reviewed
      did not establish its funding status.

      Eventee's source code is proprietary and the platform is cloud-only. It
      has a sales office in New York, and the engineering team is based in
      Europe. The site carries GDPR and ISO 27001 badges.

      Chobble Tickets is run by one person as a
      [Community Interest Company](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/community-interest-companies-introduction)
      (CIC), a UK legal structure that locks the company's assets for
      community benefit. All of Chobble's code is public under AGPLv3, and the
      platform can be self-hosted by anyone without depending on Chobble as a
      company.
