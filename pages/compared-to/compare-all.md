      ## Example: 200 tickets at £10

      The sliders start at 200 tickets a year at £10 each, which is £2,000 of
      ticket sales. On Chobble Tickets the cost is the £50 annual hosting fee
      plus Stripe's 1.5% + 20p on each payment, which comes to £120 and leaves
      £1,880. On Eventbrite's UK rate of 6.95% + £0.59 a ticket (checked 8
      August 2026), which includes payment processing, the fees are £257 and the organiser keeps £1,743.

      At very low volumes the order changes. A per-ticket platform can cost
      less than £50 a year in total, and the calculator shows that when you
      move the ticket slider down.

      ## How fees are calculated

      Each platform card shows the amount you keep after all fees are deducted
      from your total ticket sales for a year. Fees include annual subscription
      costs, per-ticket platform fees, and payment processing charges.

      The estimate assumes one ticket per payment transaction. A booking with
      several tickets usually incurs a fixed payment-processing charge once,
      so its real cost may be lower. The calculator cannot represent every
      minimum fee, cap, VAT rule, pricing tier, negotiated rate or optional
      buyer-paid charge. The comparison pages linked from the cards list fee
      sources and review dates where available.

      Platforms are sorted by total cost, with the lowest fees first, and
      Chobble Tickets is highlighted in purple. Chobble runs Chobble Tickets
      and also built this calculator.

      Click **Compare** on any platform to see a detailed side-by-side breakdown.

      You can also find Chobble Tickets on
      [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/chobble-tickets/)
      to see how it compares with other ticketing software.
