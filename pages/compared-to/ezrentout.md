      # Chobble Tickets vs EZRentOut

      EZRentOut is an operations system for rental businesses with serialised equipment, maintenance and work orders. Chobble Tickets is a booking site for one operator's dated hire and events, at a much lower fixed price.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Open source and low cost EZRentOut alternative

      EZRentOut is made by EZ Web Enterprises, Inc., which trades as EZO
      and is based in the United States. It is built for equipment rental
      operations: a catalogue of serialised assets, availability, service
      and maintenance, work orders and a rental webstore. It is a hosted
      service.

      Chobble Tickets is a booking and ticketing platform from a UK
      community interest company. Its [source code](/features/open-source/)
      is published under AGPL-3.0-only, and it can be self-hosted. It counts
      capacity per listing and per date, but it does not track individual
      assets or their maintenance history.

      EZRentOut includes tools for managing individual assets and service
      work. Chobble Tickets offers a public booking page and a fixed price.

      | Plan | Price | Billing |

      | Growth | $399/month | Billed annually |

      Both published plans include unlimited inventory and orders. Premium
      adds QuickBooks integration, sub-renting, work orders and long-term
      rental billing. Enterprise adds multiple sites, custom integrations

      Chobble Tickets is £50/year or £5/month, or £25/year or £2.50/month
      for charities, community groups, artists and musicians. It has no
      Chobble charge per booking and no per-user fee. Stripe, Square or
      SumUp processing is separate.

      EZRentOut's Growth plan costs $4,788 a year before add-ons or payment
      fees, while Chobble Tickets costs £50 a year. EZRentOut includes asset
      records and maintenance tools; work orders are listed on Premium.

      ## Data and hosting

      EZRentOut hosts asset records, maintenance history and customer data.
      The pricing page reviewed does not say whether customers can host the
      software themselves or obtain a source licence.

      Chobble Tickets encrypts personal data at rest and publishes its
      source. The operator can export the database and run the same code
      elsewhere, or self-host it. Chobble has no admin access to an
      organiser's site.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## When EZRentOut is the better choice

      - Every asset needs a serial number, location and service history
      - You run a workshop, repair jobs or planned maintenance
      - You sub-rent stock from partners
      - You bill long-term hires in cycles
      - You need B2B accounts and business-level pricing
      - You use GPS or telematics on heavy equipment
      - You need barcode scanning and purchase orders

      ## When Chobble Tickets is the better choice

      - You count stock by the day and do not need an asset record for each
        unit
      - You want a published low price with no per-user fee
      - You also sell event tickets, classes or free registrations
      - You want open source code and the option to self-host
      - Encrypted customer data matters to you
      - You need a public website beside the booking pages
