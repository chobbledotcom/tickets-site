      # Chobble Tickets vs Goodshuffle Pro

      Goodshuffle Pro runs an event rental warehouse: inventory, quotes, contracts, dispatch, staff and payments. Chobble Tickets is a public booking site for dated hire and events. They meet at the customer's booking, then go different ways.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Open source and low cost Goodshuffle Pro alternative

      Goodshuffle Pro is event rental software from Goodshuffle, Inc. in
      Washington, DC. It is built for party, tent, AV, floral and event
      rental companies: inventory with conflict detection, quotes,
      contracts, pull sheets, dispatch and payments. It is hosted,
      proprietary software.

      Chobble Tickets is a booking and ticketing platform from a UK
      community interest company. The product's
      [source code](/features/open-source/) is open under AGPL-3.0-only,
      and an operator can self-host it. It takes
      dated bookings and payments, but it does not serialise inventory,
      produce contracts or plan dispatch routes.

      Goodshuffle Pro includes warehouse and delivery planning tools.
      Chobble Tickets offers a public booking page at a fixed price.

      |---|---|---|
      | Lite | $39 | $39 |

      Lite includes unlimited projects, quotes, contracts and inventory

      digital pull sheets and dispatch with one vehicle. Extra full users
      cost $39 or $49 a month, limited users $19, and extra dispatch

      Chobble Tickets is £50/year or £5/month, or £25/year or £2.50/month

      is separate.

      Goodshuffle Pro's Lite plan is $468 a year before extra users or
      payment fees. Chobble Tickets is £50 a year. Goodshuffle includes
      inventory and contracts; dispatch is listed on Standard.

      ## Data and hosting

      Goodshuffle Pro is hosted software for inventory, client and order
      data. Its terms reserve ownership of the service to Goodshuffle and
      its licensors. The reviewed pricing page does not describe a
      self-hosted edition.

      Chobble Tickets encrypts personal data at rest and publishes its
      source. The operator can export the database and run the same code
      elsewhere, or self-host it. Chobble has no admin access to an
      organiser's site.

      ## When Goodshuffle Pro is the better choice

      - You run a warehouse with a large rental catalogue
      - You need item-level conflict detection and pull sheets
      - You plan delivery routes and assign vehicles

      - You need separate office, warehouse and driver roles
      - You want access to an event rental marketplace

      ## When Chobble Tickets is the better choice

      - You count stock per date and do not need item-level records
      - You want customers to book and pay on a public page
      - You also sell event tickets, classes or free registrations

      - You want open source code and the option to self-host
      - Encrypted customer data matters to you
