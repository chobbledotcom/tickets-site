      # Chobble Tickets vs Bookwhen

      Bookwhen takes bookings for classes, courses and events. Its plans have monthly booking allowances and different features, while Chobble Tickets charges one prepaid hosting price for its full product.
  - type: markdown

      ## Pricing and booking limits

      [Bookwhen's pricing page](https://bookwhen.com/pricing) lists the
      following UK prices, checked on 10 September 2026. The amounts below
      include the VAT shown on that page; payment processing costs extra.

      | Bookwhen plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Bookings per month | Team members | Booking pages |
      |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
      | Free | £0 | £0 | 50 | 1 | 1 |
      | Lite | £14.40 | £144 | 300 | 2 | 1 |
      | Standard | £22.80 | £228 | 800 | 5 | 3 |
      | Plus | £46.80 | £468 | 1,800 | 10 | 6 |

      Free supports free events only. Lite adds payments, discounts, ticket
      transfers and Zoom integration. Standard adds memberships, class
      passes, vouchers, reminders, waiting lists and attendance tracking.

      Plus adds telephone support by requested callback alongside online
      support. Chobble provides written support rather than a telephone
      support centre.

      Bookwhen also lists custom Enterprise plans and offers charities and
      non-profits a 20% discount, arranged through support before subscribing.
      Its [plan guide](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/6544604-choosing-a-bookwhen-plan)
      describes a Gold tier with 4,000 monthly bookings, but the public pricing
      page does not give a price for it.

      A booking means one transaction, not one ticket or attendance. Ten
      people booked together, or several class dates booked in one checkout,
      count as one booking against the allowance.

      [Bookwhen's usage guide](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/9876134-checking-subscription-statistics)
      says temporary booking overuse does not interrupt service. Continued
      overuse may lead to a request to upgrade; Bookwhen does not automatically
      upgrade the plan.

      Chobble's [managed hosting](/pricing/) costs £50/year or £5/month,
      including updates, backup tools, support and all current features.
      There are no monthly booking limits, feature tiers or per-sale platform
      fees. Charities, community groups, artists and musicians pay £25/year;
      this discount does not apply to monthly billing.

      Bookwhen's free plan costs less than Chobble's managed hosting for
      organisers whose free events fit its limits. For paid events,
      Chobble's annual hosting costs £94 less than Bookwhen Lite's £144
      annual price including VAT, before payment processing.

      ## Payment processing

      Bookwhen lists no separate per-ticket platform fee, but online payments
      carry processing charges. Its [updated Stripe integration](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/16412765-bookwhen-payments-powered-by-stripe-fee-pricing)
      uses rates set by Bookwhen, so Stripe's standard pricing may not apply.
      The published UK rates include 1.5% + 20p for standard UK cards and
      2.8% + 20p for premium UK cards.

      Bookwhen says accounts using Stripe before 26 August 2026 are likely
      still on its legacy integration, where Stripe sets the rates, with
      migration planned. PayPal is another option with its own processing
      fees. Free bookings and bookings marked as paid offline carry no
      processing charge from Bookwhen.

      Chobble Tickets sends ticket income directly to your own Stripe,
      Square or SumUp account. The chosen provider charges its normal rates,
      separate from Chobble's hosting price.

      ## Classes and repeat bookings

      Both platforms support recurring events, multiple ticket types,
      booking questions, confirmation emails and booking exports.
      Bookwhen also has tools for running an ongoing class programme:

      - **Memberships:** collect recurring membership payments and offer
        exclusive tickets on Standard and higher plans. Chobble Tickets can
        collect a [one-off membership payment](/features/purchasable-events/),
        but does not automatically renew it.
      - **Class passes:** sell credits that customers redeem against future
        classes on Standard and higher plans. Chobble's
        [packages](/features/packages/) book specified listings together,
        rather than providing credits for later use.
      - **Reminders and waiting lists:** Standard and higher plans can send
        scheduled reminders and notify people when a place becomes available.
        Chobble Tickets has [organiser-sent bulk email](/features/bulk-email/),
        but no built-in scheduled reminders or waiting list.
      - **Online classes:** Lite and higher plans can create Zoom meetings
        automatically. Standard and higher plans also support protected
        YouTube and Vimeo event pages. Chobble organisers can send an external
        meeting link by email, but it has no built-in Zoom meeting creation.

      ## Branding, websites and integrations

      Bookwhen's paid plans let you add your logo, header and colours to a
      [hosted booking page](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/753349-how-to-customise-your-public-page).
      You can embed the schedule in an existing website. Its
      [URL guide](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/3077742-how-to-change-your-public-page-url)
      lets you choose an address under `bookwhen.com`; Bookwhen does not
      offer hosting on your own domain.

      Full removal of Bookwhen branding is not documented in its
      customisation guide. Chobble Tickets includes
      [your own domain](/features/custom-domain/),
      [organiser-only branding](/features/white-labelling/) and a
      [public website](/features/your-public-website/) with content pages,
      news posts and a contact form.

      Both platforms publish an API, a way for other software to connect to
      the platform. Bookwhen's [documented API](https://api.bookwhen.com/v2)
      reads public event data. Chobble's [admin API](/features/admin-api/)
      also lets other software create, edit and delete events.

      ## Attendee data and moving providers

      Bookwhen stores booking data as a service provider acting on the
      organiser's instructions. Its [data processing agreement](https://bookwhen.com/data-processing-agreement)
      prohibits selling that personal data or using it for unrelated
      commercial purposes. This is separate from its visitor and
      account-holder privacy policy, which describes Bookwhen's own marketing.

      You can export Bookwhen customer lists, bookings and attendances as
      CSV files, which open in a spreadsheet. Customer exports include
      marketing opt-ins. The agreement allows Bookwhen to access personal
      data to solve technical problems and support queries or keep the
      service working.

      Chobble Tickets [encrypts stored names, emails and free-text answers](/features/encrypted/).
      Reading them requires credentials for an authorised user with a key,
      including a recovery owner if you enable one. Operational details such
      as dates, quantities and prices remain readable by the application.

      Chobble Tickets lets you download a [complete database backup](/features/backups/)
      and export event setup as a versioned JSON file, a structured file
      another Chobble Tickets host can import. You can run the same
      [published product](/features/open-source/) on your own infrastructure
      with no Chobble software fee; infrastructure and configured services
      cost extra. Bookwhen is not open source and does not offer a
      self-hosted edition.

      ## Ending a paid term

      Bookwhen allows cancellation at any time without a cancellation fee.
      Its plan guide says downgrading to Free keeps paid features until the
      paid period ends, and annual plans are not refundable. On leaving the
      service, its data processing agreement gives you 30 days to request
      return or deletion of personal data in writing.

      Chobble uses [prepaid hosting](/features/prepay-hosting/), not automatic
      subscription renewal. It does not store card details to renew a term.
      If the term ends without renewal, the site enters read-only mode for
      24 months, with full read access and exports but no new bookings.

      You can restart payments during that period without paying for the
      read-only months. After 24 months in read-only mode, Chobble deletes
      the site and database.
