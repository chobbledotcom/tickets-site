      # Sell event tickets and take bookings

      Chobble Tickets gives an event organiser a booking website that sells tickets online and pays the money into the organiser's own Stripe, Square or SumUp account. Managed hosting costs £50 a year or £5 a month, and Chobble takes nothing from each ticket sold.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## How an online ticket sale works

      You create a listing with a name, price and number of places. Customers
      book on your site, at your [own domain](/features/custom-domain/) or a
      free tix.chobble.net address, and pay through the payment provider you
      connected. The customer gets a ticket with a [QR
      code](/features/qr-code-check-ins/), and the booking appears in your
      attendee list with any answers to your booking questions.

      The payment goes straight from the customer to your payment account.
      Chobble never holds ticket money. Your payment provider charges its own processing fee, and the [no
      per-ticket fees page](/features/no-per-ticket-fees/) works through what
      that adds up to at 50, 500 and 5,000 tickets a year.

      ## Organisers selling tickets this way

      [Crumpsall Folk Club](https://tickets.crumpsallfolkclub.co.uk) in
      Manchester sells its monthly club nights from
      tickets.crumpsallfolkclub.co.uk. Each night has normal, solidarity and
      concession tickets as separate listings, plus raffle tickets for the
      half-time draw. The British Bourbon Society and Mane Events moved to
      Chobble Tickets from platforms that took a share of each sale, and their
      accounts are on the [reviews page](/reviews/).

      ## What Chobble Tickets does not do

      Chobble Tickets has no event marketplace, so it does not show your
      events to people browsing for something to do. Customers reach your
      booking site through your own website, social media and mailing list.
      It also has no seating plans, so it sells against a number of places
      rather than named seats.
