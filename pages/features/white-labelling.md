      # White labelling

      Booking pages, tickets and emails carry the organiser's name and no Chobble branding. There is no "powered by" link, no higher tier and no add-on to buy.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## What white labelling means here

      A white label product carries the name of the organisation using it
      rather than the name of the company that made it. Attendees who book a
      ticket see the organiser's site, and need not know which software
      produced it.

      Chobble Tickets is white label on every site, including the £50/year
      managed plan. Some platforms sell branding removal as a paid add-on or
      restrict it to a higher plan.

      The public site carries no Chobble branding anywhere. The only Chobble
      link in the product is in the footer of the administration area, which
      only logged-in staff see.

      ## Where the organiser's name appears

      - **The web address.** Every managed site gets a
        `yourname.tix.chobble.net` address, and a
        [custom domain](/features/custom-domain/) such as
        `tickets.example.org` can be pointed at it by CNAME at no extra
        charge.
      - **The public site.** The
        [built-in website](/features/your-public-website/) uses the site title,
        header image and content the organiser writes, with
        [light and dark themes](/features/light-and-dark-themes/) and custom
        CSS for colours, fonts and spacing.
      - **Emails.** Confirmations are sent through the organiser's account with
        [Resend, Postmark, SendGrid or Mailgun](/features/email-providers/), so
        they come from the organiser's own domain. The subject, HTML body and
        text body are [editable templates](/features/email-templates/).
      - **Tickets and check-in.** Ticket pages and QR codes sit on the
        organiser's domain alongside the rest of the site.
      - **An existing website.** The
        [embeddable widget](/features/embeddable-widget/) and
        [order widget](/features/order-widget/) put booking inside a site the
        organiser already runs.

      ## No attribution requirement

      Chobble Tickets is published under the
      [AGPL-3.0-only licence](/features/open-source/). The licence has no
      "powered by" or visible attribution term, so a self-hosted copy can
      remove or replace the branding without buying a commercial licence.

      Self-hosting has no Chobble software fee. The operator pays for
      infrastructure, email and payment processing instead.

      ## No marketplace and no attendee marketing

      Chobble Tickets has no public event marketplace, so listings are not

      attendees about other events or build audience profiles from them, as
      described in [no attendee marketing](/features/no-spying/) and
      [who can see your data](/features/who-can-see-your-data/).
