      ## Hire lengths and stock

      Create a [daily listing](/features/daily-events/) for each hot tub model and set its capacity to the number you own. If you have three four-seat tubs and two six-seat tubs, that is two listings with capacities of three and two.

      Publish a total price for each hire length, such as three days for £250 or seven days for £450. [Multi-day hire](/features/multi-day-hire/) checks the whole range before payment and again when payment confirmation arrives.

      A paid booking uses a tub only when the payment is confirmed. If two customers pay for the last tub on the same dates, the later payment is refunded automatically. Opening checkout does not hold the tub.
  - type: split-icon-links

      ## Delivery, collection and access

      [Logistics](/features/logistics/) assigns the drop-off and collection to a driver or crew. Each agent sees the address, phone number and a map link on their own run sheet, then marks the leg done.

      Ask for access details at checkout with [custom questions](/features/custom-questions/). A question can cover the surface the tub will stand on, the power supply and whether the customer has a garden hose. Free-text answers are encrypted.

      [Postcode lookup](/features/postcode-lookup/) reduces address typing, and staff can check the address before assigning the job.

      ## Cleaning and servicing between hires

      Create a [servicing event](/features/servicing-events/) to hold one place on the listing during cleaning and water checks. Other tubs remain available. Check the calendar first, as a servicing hold does not move or cancel existing bookings.

      The servicing event can carry the cost of chemicals, a replacement filter or a repair. The cost posts to the [ledger](/features/ledger/) against the listing, so the listing's profit reflects the work.

      ## Taking a damage deposit

      Chobble Tickets can take part of the hire price at booking and the balance later. That is a payment plan. It is not a refundable security deposit.

      To take a damage deposit, add a mandatory surcharge or booking fee at checkout. The customer pays it with the hire. After collection and inspection, return the unused amount in the Stripe, Square or SumUp dashboard.

      Chobble Tickets does not pre-authorise a card or hold the money itself. Partial refunds are made in the payment provider's dashboard, not in the Chobble admin panel, so the ledger will not record the returned amount automatically. The [hire deposits guide](/guide/choosing-hire-software/security-deposits-for-hire/) sets out the trade-off.

      ## Worked example

      A hire company owns three four-seat tubs and charges £250 for three days. A customer books Friday to Sunday. The booking uses one tub on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

      Collection appears on Sunday's run sheet, the last booked day. With Monday and Tuesday still free, the company creates a two-day servicing hold for one tub and records £18 of chemicals against the listing. That place becomes available again on Wednesday.

      The company adds a £100 damage deposit as a mandatory charge and returns it after the tub is checked.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## What this setup does not do

      - It does not track tubs by serial number or keep a service history for each one.
      - It does not hold or pre-authorise a refundable damage deposit.
      - It does not calculate delivery zones or optimise routes.
      - It does not book by the hour. Half-day sessions need separate grouped listings, with capacity managed carefully.
      - It does not record water test readings.

      A rental operations system is the better fit if every tub needs its own maintenance record and parts history.
