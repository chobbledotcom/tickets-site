      # Ticketing for scare attractions

      Sell tickets for haunted houses, scare trails, and Halloween events. Each night has its own capacity, and managed hosting is £50/year, whatever the season's sales.
  - type: split-image
    content: |
      ## A short season with its own calendar

      [Daily events](/features/daily-events/) run an attraction across its
      season, with a separate capacity for each night and blackout dates
      for the nights it is closed. Visitors pick their night from a
      calendar.

      Chobble Tickets does not provide a timetable where visitors pick any
      clock time, so timed entry works by publishing each entry session as
      its own listing, such as "19:00 entry" and "20:00 entry", each with
      its own capacity. Peak nights can run as a separate listing with a
      higher price, set to the weekdays it covers.

      Capacity is used only when payment confirmation arrives. If
      confirmed payments race for the last places on a night, the later
      order is refunded automatically.

      The same setup covers scare mazes, haunted houses, Halloween trails,
      ghost tours and seasonal horror events.

      ## Entry terms and the gate

      [Terms and conditions](/features/terms-and-conditions/) require every
      buyer to tick a checkbox before booking, which covers minimum age
      rules, health warnings, and conduct policies. A special instructions
      field collects anything visitors need to tell you in advance.

      At the gate, staff
      [scan tickets through a browser with a phone camera](/features/qr-code-check-ins/),
      with no scanner hardware. The scanner flags tickets that were
      already scanned, and a name search covers anyone whose screen will
      not scan.

      The £50/year price covers the whole year even when the attraction
      trades for a few weeks each autumn, and there is no per-ticket
      platform fee on top. Ticket income goes directly to your own Stripe,
      Square, or SumUp account.
