      ## Concerts, fundraisers and groups

      A concert, carol service, quiz night or fundraising dinner becomes a
      listing with its own capacity for each date. Tickets can be sold at a
      fixed price, left as pay-what-you-want, or free with a limit on
      places.

      Regular sessions such as toddler groups, coffee mornings and holiday
      clubs use one listing with per-date capacity, so each session fills
      separately. A session with no charge works as a
      [sign-up form](/features/sign-up-forms/) and needs no payment
      provider.

      [Custom questions](/features/custom-questions/) can ask about
      dietary needs at a fundraising meal, or collect helper availability
      on a volunteer rota. Answers appear with each booking and in the
      [CSV exports](/features/csv-export/).

      ## Running the event

      Each booking receives a QR code by email. Volunteers can
      [check people in with a phone camera](/features/qr-code-check-ins/)
      or find a booking by name, and walk-ins can be added at the door.

      Money from ticket sales, door income and the costs of an event are
      recorded in one [ledger](/features/ledger/), which gives a treasurer
      a report without a separate spreadsheet. Attendee contact details
      are [encrypted in the database](/features/encrypted/), and the
      church chooses which contact fields each event collects.

      [Bulk email](/features/bulk-email/) can tell everyone booked on a
      listing about a cancellation or a venue change, sent through the
      church's own email provider.

      ## What it does not do

      Chobble Tickets has no event discovery marketplace, so a concert is
      not listed beside other organisers' events. The church promotes its
      own events through its site, notices and mailing list.

      Chobble Tickets books whole days and ranges of days. It does not
      offer hourly slots, so a room let by the hour to different groups
      cannot be represented here.

      Paid checkout does not reserve capacity. Capacity is used only when
      payment confirmation arrives.
