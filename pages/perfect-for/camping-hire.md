      ## Tents, kit and hire lengths

      Each tent type is a [daily listing](/features/daily-events/) with a capacity equal to the number you own. A four-person bell tent and a six-person bell tent are separate listings, each with its own price and count.

      Publish a total price for each hire length. For example, a three-day hire from Friday to Sunday might cost £180. [Multi-day hire](/features/multi-day-hire/) counts calendar days, including both the first and last day. Describe any overnight stays separately so customers know when to expect collection.

      A paid booking uses a tent when payment confirmation arrives. If two payments reach the last tent for the same dates, the later one is refunded automatically. Checkout does not reserve it.
  - type: split-icon-links

      ## Add the extras at checkout

      Use [add-ons](/features/promo-codes-and-add-ons/) for camp kitchens, bedding, lighting, extra chairs and firewood. Each add-on can have its own stock limit and price. Check whether a stock limit suits your operation: it is not a separate per-day hire inventory for each extra.

      If a tent requires a particular extra, a [parent/child listing](/features/parent-child-listings/) can make one listing depend on another. A bell tent can require a bedding pack, for example, so the customer cannot check out without choosing one.

      A group of listings can also be sold on one page, which suits a package of tent, bedding and cooking kit.

      ## Delivery, collection and site details

      [Logistics](/features/logistics/) assigns each drop-off and collection to a driver or crew. The run sheet shows the address, phone number and a map link, and the crew marks each leg done.

      [Custom questions](/features/custom-questions/) can collect the pitch number, site name, arrival time and whether the customer has a vehicle. Answers are required before checkout and free-text answers are encrypted.

      [Postcode lookup](/features/postcode-lookup/) helps the customer enter a complete address, which staff can review before assigning the delivery.

      ## Taking a damage deposit

      A Chobble deposit is a part-payment towards the hire price. The customer pays some of the price at booking and the rest through a balance link. That is not a refundable security deposit.

      For a damage deposit, add a mandatory fixed-price modifier or a percentage-based booking fee to the order. A part-payment can leave some of a fixed surcharge in the balance. After the kit is checked, return the unused amount from the Stripe, Square or SumUp dashboard.

      Chobble Tickets does not pre-authorise a card or hold the deposit. Partial refunds happen in the payment provider's dashboard, not in the Chobble admin panel, so the ledger does not reconcile them automatically. The [hire deposits guide](/guide/choosing-hire-software/security-deposits-for-hire/) explains the accounting.

      ## Worked example

      A company has ten four-person bell tents and charges £180 for three days, Friday to Sunday. Eight bookings cover those dates, leaving two tents. A ninth booking leaves one tent. If two payments race for that last tent, the later order is refunded.

      The company adds a £75 damage deposit as a mandatory charge. Customers can choose a bedding pack and a camp kitchen as add-ons. Drivers see the Friday drop-offs and Sunday collections on their run sheets.

      The company does not assign a numbered tent to a customer. It counts how many tents of each type are available.

      ## What this setup does not do

      - It does not allocate a numbered pitch or a specific tent.
      - It does not track individual tents or kit by serial number.
      - It does not hold or pre-authorise a refundable damage deposit.
      - It does not offer an arbitrary hourly time picker. Fixed hire times can be separate named listings in a group, but do not check overlaps with full-day hires.
      - It does not calculate delivery zones or optimise routes.

      A rental operations system is the better fit when every tent has its own cleaning, repair and parts history.
