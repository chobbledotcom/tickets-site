  - type: split-image

      ## Day tickets and weekend passes

      Saturday and Sunday day tickets are separate listings in one
      [group](/features/groups/), which lists them on one public page. A
      combined booking link from the dashboard books both in one form and
      one payment. Each day's listing carries its own capacity, so a
      sold-out Saturday does not stop Sunday sales.

      A weekend pass sells as a [package](/features/packages/) of one
      Saturday and one Sunday admission, so each pass takes a place from
      both days and scans at each day's door.

      [Promo codes](/features/promo-codes-and-add-ons/) with stock limits
      handle early-bird pricing, where a discount applies to a fixed number
      of orders and then stops.
      [Custom questions](/features/custom-questions/) collect accessibility
      requirements or cosplay details at checkout.

      A convention selling 3,000 tickets pays £50 for the year. A platform
      charging £1 per ticket would collect £3,000 from the same sales.

      ## Traders, queues, and attendee data

      Trader and exhibitor pitches sell as their own listings, with
      [custom questions](/features/custom-questions/) collecting the stall
      name and what will be sold. For larger pitches,
      [deposits](/features/deposits-and-balance-payments/) take part of the
      fee at booking and send a link to pay the balance later.

      At the doors, each authorised staff member
      [scans tickets with their own phone](/features/qr-code-check-ins/),
      so several entry lanes can run at once. The scanner flags duplicate
      tickets, and visitors can keep their ticket in
      [Apple Wallet or Google Wallet](/features/apple-wallet/).

      Chobble Tickets is [white-labelled](/features/white-labelling/) and
      has no event marketplace, so attendees see the convention's branding
      and receive no marketing from Chobble. Attendee data is
      [encrypted at rest](/features/encrypted/).
