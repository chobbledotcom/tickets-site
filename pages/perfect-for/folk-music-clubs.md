      ## Account for advance sales and money at the door

      A repeating listing can cover the regular club night, while a fixed-date listing can cover a guest concert. Admission can have a fixed price, be free or let the buyer choose an amount above a minimum.

      The [ledger](/features/ledger/) records checkout payments and refunds. A club owner can add cash, card-machine takings, bank transfers, venue costs and performer costs, giving each club night a statement and net balance.

      Money date ranges follow the date each transaction was recorded. The calendar and attendee list provide the roster for a selected club night.

      ## Sell admission and raffle entries

      A [purchasable listing](/features/purchasable-events/) sells raffle entries without creating a ticket that needs to be checked in. Buyers can choose a quantity, and the public order page can place admission and raffle entries in one order.

      Chobble Tickets records the sale but does not choose or record a raffle winner. The draw remains with the club.

      On the door, staff can scan ticket QR codes, search by name or add walk-ins. Attendance and payment records can be exported for the treasurer.
