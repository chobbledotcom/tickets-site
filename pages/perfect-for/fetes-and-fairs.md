      ## Admission, stalls, and the raffle in one place

      Adult, child, and family admission run as separate listings in a

      [custom questions](/features/custom-questions/) collecting the stall

      [deposit at booking](/features/deposits-and-balance-payments/) with
      the balance paid later by emailed link.

      [purchasable events](/features/purchasable-events/), with quantity
      selection and a capacity cap on the number of entries. The draw
      itself runs outside Chobble Tickets.

      ## On the gate and in the books

      Volunteers [scan tickets with a phone camera](/features/qr-code-check-ins/)
      at the gate, and cash sales on the day are added as
      [manual attendees](/features/manual-attendees/), so the visitor
      count stays complete.

      The [ledger](/features/ledger/) records advance sales, gate cash,
      and refunds in one place, with a statement per listing. A committee
      can hand the treasurer one set of figures that matches every page
      in the admin area.

      Chobble Tickets charges no per-ticket platform fee, so small margins
      stay with the event. Charities and community groups pay £25/year,
      and ticket income goes directly to the group's own Stripe, Square,
      or SumUp account.
