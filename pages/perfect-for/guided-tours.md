      # Booking software for guided tours

      Take bookings for walking tours, ghost tours, food tours, and heritage tours. Each departure has its own capacity, and managed hosting costs £50/year or £5/month.
  - type: split-icon-links
    content: |
      ## Departures with small-group limits

      [Daily events](/features/daily-events/) run a tour on the days you
      choose, with a separate capacity for each date, so a walking tour
      capped at 15 people stays at 15 on every departure. Guests pick
      their date from a calendar, and holiday dates block days you are
      not running.

      Chobble Tickets does not provide a single timetable where guests
      pick any clock time, so each departure time is published as its own
      listing, such as "11:00 Old Town Tour" and "14:00 Old Town Tour".

      [Registration deadlines](/features/registration-deadlines/) close
      bookings automatically before each departure, so you know the group
      size before you set off. A purchase limit caps how many places one
      person can book.

      The same setup covers walking tours, ghost walks, brewery and
      distillery tours, museum tours, wildlife walks and boat trips.

      ## At the meeting point and when plans change

      [Custom questions](/features/custom-questions/) collect what a guide
      needs to know in advance, such as mobility requirements, language
      preference, or dietary requirements on a food tour. Answers appear
      in the attendee list and in [CSV exports](/features/csv-export/).

      At the meeting point, guides
      [check guests in by phone scan or name search](/features/qr-code-check-ins/),
      and walk-ups are added as
      [manual attendees](/features/manual-attendees/). Booking
      confirmations are sent by email, and tickets can be saved to
      [Apple Wallet or Google Wallet](/features/apple-wallet/).

      If weather cancels a departure, each booking for that date can be
      [refunded](/features/refunds/) from the admin panel, and
      [bulk email](/features/bulk-email/) sends one message to the
      tour's booked guests, skipping anyone who has unsubscribed.
