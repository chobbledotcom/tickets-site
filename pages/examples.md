      [Chalkie's School for Dogs](https://www.chalkiesdogs.co.uk) sells dog
      training classes from its own domain. Listings have included "Total
      Recall (whistle)" and "Sunday School".

      Chalkie's Dogs pays £40 a month for hosting with support. That arrangement

      Console setup, and priority for its feature requests. The ticketing
      features are the same as on every other hosted site, and managed hosting
      on its own costs £5 a month or £50 a year.

      ## Demo sites

      These demos show how Chobble Tickets fits different kinds of business.
      Each one runs on a supplied chobble.net address, the kind of address every
      hosted site starts on. A hosted site can also serve an organiser's own
      domain, as the two customer sites above do.

      ### Chobble Arts Workshops

      [Chobble Arts Workshops](https://workshop.tix.chobble.net/) is a demo site
      for a business that runs arts classes. Its listings include pottery wheel
      classes, life drawing evenings, printmaking, watercolour sessions and art
      clubs for children.

      ### Chobble Skip Hire

      [Chobble Skip Hire](https://skips.tix.chobble.net/) is a demo site for a
      skip hire business. Each skip size from two to ten yards is its own
      listing, so customers book the size they need.

      ### Chobble Play Centre

      [Chobble Play Centre](https://playcentre.tix.chobble.net/) is a demo site
      for an indoor play centre. It sells open play sessions, toddler mornings,
      trampoline sessions, private party room hire and family packages.
