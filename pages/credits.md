      # Credits

      Chobble Tickets is built by one developer at Chobble CIC. It runs on
      open source software, and the organisers listed below shaped what got
      built and in what order.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Who builds it

      [Stef Burke](/about/) writes and maintains Chobble Tickets. He has
      worked in software since around 2007, spent five years on the payments
      team at Bandcamp, and ten years as lead developer at Bouncy Castle
      Network, where he wrote the booking software the company ran on.

      Chobble CIC, company number 17050113, employs him. The community
      interest company structure puts an asset lock on the business, and the
      product is published under the AGPL-3.0-only licence. A future owner
      could not undo either. [Why Chobble Tickets cannot go closed
      source](/features/cannot-go-closed-source/) sets out the mechanics.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Open source it builds on

      No one outside the team has contributed code so far. Like all open
      source software, Chobble Tickets stands on other people's work:

      - The [Chobble
        Template](https://github.com/chobbledotcom/chobble-template), the
        Eleventy-based site system this website is built with
      - The [Chobble Tickets
        repository](https://github.com/chobbledotcom/tickets), the booking
        platform behind every hosted site
      - The many libraries both projects depend on, whose authors are named
        in their licence files
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Organisers who shaped it

      The organisers below used the platform in its first months. Their
      requests and bug reports decided what got built.

      Thanks to:

      - **Spencer, Elliott's Bouncy Castles.** Pushed Chobble to build
        Chobble Tickets out of annoyance with the existing options, and wrote
        the initial specification.
      - **Ben, Jersey Girls.** Gave the first feedback on the project from a
        developer's point of view.
      - **Chalkie's Dogs**
        ([chalkiesdogs.co.uk](https://www.chalkiesdogs.co.uk)). The first
        paying customer, on the [hosting with support](/support/) package,
        and suggested the [hidden pages](/features/hidden-pages/) feature.
      - **Danny, Oxford University Electronic Music Society.** Suggested the
        group check-ins and clearer email receipt features, and reported bugs
        that have since been fixed.
      - **Alan, Mane Events.** Helped Chobble debug the embedded forms.
