    content: |
      Hire software needs to count availability across a date range and
      price a hire length. Some operators also need deposits, deliveries
      and collections. These guides compare prices, stock control and fit.

      ## Where to start

      The [rental software costs
      guide](/guide/choosing-hire-software/rental-booking-software-costs/)
      puts the published prices beside Chobble Tickets and shows what a
      three-year bill can include beyond the monthly headline. The
      [single-operator or
      marketplace](/guide/choosing-hire-software/single-operator-or-marketplace/)
      guide explains why a marketplace script is a different purchase from a
      booking site.

      If you already know you need a booking site, the [day-based hire
      checklist](/guide/choosing-hire-software/day-based-hire-software/)
      covers stock, half-days, deliveries and data. The [hire deposits
      guide](/guide/choosing-hire-software/security-deposits-for-hire/)
      separates a part-payment from a refundable damage deposit.

      ## How these guides are written

      Chobble, the community interest company that runs Chobble Tickets,
      writes these guides and appears in them as one of the products.
      Prices are dated and linked to provider sources. The guides also
      explain where Chobble Tickets does not fit.
